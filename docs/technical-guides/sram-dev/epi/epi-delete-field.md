# EPI | Delete Field

Summary - In some cases where development work has been done around removing a field in EPI, that field still remains until it’s deleted / removed through Optimizely (EPI). The following are instructions on how to do this. 

<br>

## Steps
1. Login to Optimizely / EPI

1. Click - Admin 

1. Click - Content Type tab
![Image from images folder](/sram-dev/epi/delete-field/opt_field-not-removed_content-type.png)
<br>

1. Expand the “Block Types” section

1. Find “Contact Us Social Media” - Click it
![Image from images folder](/sram-dev/epi/delete-field/opt_field-not-removed_contact-us-social-media.png)
<br>

1. Notice the list of fields 
![Image from images folder](/sram-dev/epi/delete-field/opt_field-not-removed_list-of-fields.png)  
<br>

1. Find the TwitterUrl field - Click it
![Image from images folder](/sram-dev/epi/delete-field/opt_field-not-removed_select-twitterurl.png)
<br>

1. Edit Properties window will open 

1. Click - Delete
![Image from images folder](/sram-dev/epi/delete-field/opt_field-not-removed_twitterUrl-edit-property.png)
<br>

1. Notice the updated list of fields that doesn’t include TwitterUrl
![Image from images folder](/sram-dev/epi/delete-field/opt_field-not-removed_updated-list-of-fields.png)
